🤸‍♂️ Handstand

The handstand is a foundational gymnastics skill that builds shoulder strength, core stability, and body awareness. It requires balance, alignment, and consistent practice.

How to Perform:

  1. Stand facing a wall, arms raised overhead.
  2. Step forward and place both hands flat on the floor, shoulder-width apart.
  3. Kick one leg up and follow with the other so both legs reach the wall.
  4. Keep arms straight, core tight, and body in one straight line.
  5. Hold for 5–10 seconds. Lower down with control.
💡 Tips: Look at your hands, not forward. Spread your fingers wide for better balance. Practice against a wall first before attempting freestanding.

🌉 The Back Arch (Bridge)

The back arch (bridge pose) develops spinal flexibility, strengthens the back and glutes, and opens the chest and shoulders. It is a key gymnastics movement for backbends and walkovers.

How to Perform:

  1. Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat, hip-width apart.
  2. Place hands beside your ears, fingers pointing toward your shoulders.
  3. Press through your feet and hands to lift your hips toward the ceiling.
  4. Straighten your arms fully and arch your back into a bridge shape.
  5. Hold for 5–10 seconds, then slowly lower yourself back down.
💡 Tips: Warm up your spine beforehand. Keep feet parallel. If wrists hurt, build wrist flexibility separately before attempting.
Warm-Up

🧘 Stretching Exercises

Stretching improves range of motion, prevents injury, and prepares your muscles for gymnastics work. Always stretch before and after practice sessions.

Essential Stretches:

  • Seated Forward Fold: Sit with legs straight, reach forward and hold for 20 sec.
  • Butterfly Stretch: Sit with soles of feet together, press knees toward floor.
  • Shoulder Stretch: Bring one arm across your chest, hold with the other arm, 20 sec each side.
  • Hip Flexor Lunge: Step one foot forward into a lunge, lower back knee to floor. Hold 20 sec each side.
  • Cobra Pose: Lie face down, press up through hands to arch your upper back.
💡 Tips: Never bounce while stretching. Breathe steadily. Stretch only to mild discomfort, not pain.
